# -From-Servant-to-Master
A working paper on AI companions and the AI Driving License framework
From Servant to Master is an active policy and research initiative addressing the psychological shift from treating AI as a tool to treating it as a counterpart.
 
Current Status: The project is led by Ali Shafagh (Independent Researcher & Former Diplomat). A foundational working paper has been published (SSRN, 2026), and the project is currently developing its core governance framework.
 
Key Outputs:
• The "AI Driving License" Framework: A practical governance tool for training, testing, and certifying qualified AI users, complementing existing frameworks like the UNESCO AI Ethics Recommendation and the EU AI Act.
• Open Co-Creation: An open, collaborative document has been established for public co-creation of the certification criteria. 
Next Steps: Refining the certification framework through public consultation and seeking partnerships with educational and policy institutions for pilot implementation.
